Project use
-----------
This CSV is the project's source of truth and must not be modified in place.
Analysis selects EVS records with study == 1, producing 59,438 respondents
from 36 countries. Derived files, including the EVS-only Parquet dataset, are
reproducible outputs and do not replace this raw source. The documented
negative missing-value codes (-1 through -5) are preserved.
